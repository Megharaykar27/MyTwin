## Project: Brain stroke prediction (first version)
The first version was a Flask web app predicting brain stroke risk from age, gender, hypertension, heart disease, BMI, average glucose level and smoking status on a 5,140-record dataset. It handled class imbalance (4,733 vs 407 cases), compared Logistic Regression, KNN, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Naive Bayes and Extra Trees, and added registration and login with Flask-Login and SQLAlchemy. It later became NeuroCheck: while rebuilding it, Megha found hidden near-duplicate stroke rows in the dataset that made the old 83.95% accuracy look better than reality, so NeuroCheck uses leak-safe testing and reports an honest ROC-AUC of 0.84.
